# Meta AI

Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta_AI

## Overview

Meta AI is Meta Platforms’ AI research division and also the name used for its consumer assistant. It develops AI and AR technologies and is tightly integrated into Meta’s social and device ecosystem.

## History

The group started in 2013 as Facebook AI Research (FAIR). It expanded globally and participated in the Partnership on AI. Leadership included Yann LeCun and later Jérôme Pesenti. FAIR became Meta AI after Meta’s corporate rebrand.

## Research focus

Meta AI has worked on NLP, multilingual translation, chatbots, self-supervised learning, GAN-related work, and computer vision. It released important open tooling including Torch and PyTorch.

Notable model efforts include Galactica (withdrawn quickly) and the Llama family, which became central to Meta’s generative AI strategy.

## Products/models

Meta AI is deployed as an assistant across Meta products, standalone app variants, Ray-Ban Meta glasses, and Quest devices. Llama is the key model line in the article.

## Criticism/controversies

The article highlights concerns around unattributed news summarization, training-data allegations involving pirated sources, and model quality/safety issues such as the withdrawal of Galactica.

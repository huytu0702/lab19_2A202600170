# Google DeepMind

Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_DeepMind

## Overview

Google DeepMind is Alphabet’s AI research lab. It began as DeepMind in 2010, was acquired by Google in 2014, and merged with Google Brain in 2023. The lab is known for reinforcement learning, protein structure prediction, multimodal foundation models, and scientific AI.

## History

DeepMind was founded by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg, and Mustafa Suleyman. Early breakthroughs came from Atari reinforcement-learning systems. Over time, the organization expanded into science, health, infrastructure optimization, and generative AI. In 2023, DeepMind and Google Brain were consolidated as Google DeepMind.

## Research milestones

Major milestones include AlphaGo’s victories over top Go players, AlphaZero and MuZero for generalized game learning, AlphaStar for StarCraft II, and DeepNash for Stratego.

In science, AlphaFold and AlphaFold2 delivered major advances in protein structure prediction, followed by broader databases and AlphaFold3 for biomolecular interactions.

The lab also produced AlphaTensor, AlphaGeometry, AlphaProof, and AlphaDev/AlphaEvolve, showing advances in algorithm discovery and formal reasoning.

## Products and models

Google DeepMind drives Gemini (including chatbot and multimodal model families), Gemma, Imagen, Veo, and Lyria. It has also worked on robotics (RoboCat, Gemini Robotics), coding systems (AlphaCode), and simulation-oriented AI.

## Governance/ethics

The organization has operated ethics and society programs and has faced scrutiny over health-data governance in NHS-linked deployments. It has also published safety-oriented work and rule-based frameworks for robotics behavior.

# Cohere

Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohere

## Overview

Cohere is a Canadian AI company founded in 2019 and headquartered in Toronto. It focuses on enterprise-oriented large language models and NLP systems for business and government use.

## History

Founded by Aidan Gomez, Ivan Zhang, and Nick Frosst, Cohere emerged from transformer-era research backgrounds. It expanded through cloud integrations, international offices, and public policy commitments around responsible AI. Cohere Labs (formerly Cohere For AI) supports nonprofit/open research initiatives.

## Products and technology

Cohere offers generative AI models and tools for chat, search, summarization, and enterprise workflows via APIs and cloud platforms. The article references products such as Command A family, North (including banking-focused versions), and Aya Vision.

## Partnerships/funding

Cohere has raised multiple major rounds and partnered with cloud providers and enterprise vendors including Google Cloud, Oracle, Salesforce, Nvidia-linked ecosystems, and regional partners such as Fujitsu and LG.

## Issues/criticisms

The article notes a 2025 copyright lawsuit by media publishers alleging unauthorized training/content reproduction, reflecting broader legal pressures on generative AI firms regarding data sourcing and output reuse.

# Anthropic

Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthropic

## Overview

Anthropic is a private U.S. artificial intelligence company based in San Francisco. It develops the Claude family of large language models and presents itself as a company centered on AI safety, reliability, and responsible deployment. The firm was established in 2021 by former OpenAI staff, including Dario and Daniela Amodei.

## History

Anthropic was founded in 2021 by a group of former OpenAI employees who left over strategic disagreements. In 2022, Anthropic completed training its first Claude model but delayed public release for additional safety checks. Claude was released in March 2023.

In 2024 and 2025, the company expanded quickly, hired prominent researchers, announced Claude 4, introduced developer capabilities such as Model Context Protocol support, and launched Claude Code to general availability. It also made policy decisions on sales restrictions and expanded cloud and enterprise partnerships.

## Products and technology

Anthropic’s flagship product line is Claude, available via web app, API, cloud integrations, and desktop/mobile clients. A key technical approach is constitutional AI, which uses a rule-based “constitution” to guide model behavior toward helpfulness, harmlessness, and honesty.

Anthropic also emphasizes interpretability research, including studies on internal model features and multilingual conceptual processing. It offers developer-oriented and end-user-oriented tools, including Claude Code and Claude Cowork.

## Funding/partnerships

Anthropic has raised large funding rounds and built major partnerships with Amazon, Google, Microsoft/Nvidia-related infrastructure providers, and enterprise platforms such as Snowflake. Its valuation rose rapidly through 2025–2026.

## Legal and policy issues

Anthropic has faced major copyright disputes (including music and books), a Reddit lawsuit, and policy controversy over military/intelligence use restrictions. The article also describes conflicts with U.S. defense stakeholders around safeguards and deployment constraints.

# Stability AI

Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stability_AI

## Overview

Stability AI is a British AI company headquartered in London, founded in 2019. It is best known for Stable Diffusion and has expanded to models for image, video, audio, 3D, and text generation.

## History

The company gained major visibility in 2022 with the release of Stable Diffusion. In 2024, founder Emad Mostaque resigned as CEO; interim leadership was followed by appointment of Prem Akkaraju as chief executive.

## Models/products

The portfolio includes Stable Diffusion 3.5, Stable Video variants, Stable Audio, 3D model families, and language/code offerings under the Stable brand. The company has also pursued device optimization partnerships.

## Funding and business changes

Stability AI raised large funding rounds led by major investors and later reorganized leadership and board structure. Additional investment and board expansion in 2024 reflected efforts to stabilize operations and scale.

## Legal/ethical controversies

The article notes founder-equity litigation and major copyright disputes, especially with Getty Images, centered on use of copyrighted content for model training. These issues place the company in broader debates around ownership, governance, and training-data rights.

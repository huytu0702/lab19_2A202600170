# Hugging Face

Source URL: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugging_Face

## Overview

Hugging Face is a private AI company based in New York City focused on machine learning infrastructure and developer tooling. It is best known for the Transformers library and a platform for sharing models and datasets.

## History

Founded in 2016 by Clément Delangue, Julien Chaumond, and Thomas Wolf, the company began as a teen-focused chatbot project and then pivoted to open ML tooling. A major milestone was the BigScience effort and BLOOM model release. Hugging Face later partnered with AWS, joined ecosystem programs with Meta/Scaleway, and worked with UNESCO on multilingual translation initiatives.

## Products/platform

Key offerings include model hosting, dataset sharing, Spaces for demos, inference tools, and learning resources. Hugging Face serves as a distribution and collaboration hub for open and applied AI development.

## Partnerships and ecosystem

The company collaborates across cloud providers, research groups, startups, and public institutions. Recent expansion includes acquisition of Pollen Robotics, signaling broader ambitions in open robotics and embodied AI.

## Risks/controversies

The article highlights a 2026 security incident in which attackers abused platform channels for Android-targeted malware campaigns, underlining risks associated with open distribution ecosystems.
